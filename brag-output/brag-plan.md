# Brag plan: GhostAuth

## Nine-question rubric
1. **App:** Android personal-security research prototype with native fingerprint/PIN checks and user-enabled protection for selected apps.
2. **Strongest claim:** “Your apps. Your access.” Real native authentication is the impressive part; behavior learning and risk scoring remain previews and will not be advertised.
3. **Visual hook:** The approved fingerprint mark beside oversized cream-and-cobalt typography.
4. **Actual UI:** App-selection rows, the minimal fingerprint gate with PIN fallback, and the identity-confirmed screen, recreated from source for motion.
5. **Shortest satisfying cut:** 20 seconds.
6. **Tone:** polished; a precise, editorial Android product film.
7. **Audio:** Clean vol-12 music bed, quiet selection clicks, one warm verification accent. No narration.
8. **Caption:** Built GhostAuth: your apps, your access. An Android research prototype with fingerprint/PIN verification, selected-app protection, and security history stored on-device.
9. **Flow:** Choose apps → fingerprint verification (PIN fallback visible) → identity confirmed and app handoff.

## Angle and hook
Access is personal. Open with the project's own “Your apps. Your access.” copy, then make that claim concrete through a source-faithful animated product demonstration. The fingerprint mark is the recurring visual anchor, never a generic security shield.

## Format and identity
Landscape, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, 20 seconds. Paper #f5f4f0, ink #191919, cobalt #2448d8, stone #d8d6cf. Arimo regular/bold and Cousine labels, bundled from the project. Sharp rules and compact square controls echo the application. Source: index.html, README.md, src/mobile/screens.ts, state.ts, GhostAuthApp.tsx, mobile.css, and public/assets/ghostauth-logo.png.

## Storyboard
### 1 — Your access — 0–3.3 seconds
Large two-line “Your apps. Your access.” at left; actual fingerprint logo fills the right. Small GhostAuth wordmark and Android research prototype label. Fast 0.45s entrance; copy holds for over 2s. Soft music onset, no SFX. Clean transition.
### 2 — Choose — 3.3–8.74 seconds
Left: “Choose your private apps.” Right: a cream phone panel drawn from app-selection source. Mail, Photos, Files, Browser rows; sequentially select Mail and Photos; then press “Save selection.” Labels persist for reading. Cursor motion and two soft click cues show cause and effect. Small label: UI demonstration. Sequential actions use music beats at 4.39 and 5.34s, with a longer settled hold before save at 6.56s. Music continues.
### 3 — Verify — 8.74–14.4 seconds
Beat-locked scene start at strong cue 8.74s. “A familiar way in.” at left; “Fingerprint + PIN” below. Phone switches to minimal native gate, approved fingerprint mark and “Use PIN” secondary option. A touch indicator approaches the fingerprint reader; successful check resolves into the source copy “You’re verified.” at 12.02s on the beat grid, with over two seconds to read the confirmation. This is a stylized UI demonstration, not a live Android sensor recording. No invented inbox or personal data. Soft click at scan and warm low-frequency success accent. Keep result readable through 14.4s.
### 4 — GhostAuth — 14.4–20 seconds
Phone and copy draw together into a final brand composition. Approved fingerprint mark, GhostAuth, “Your apps. Your access.” and “Android research prototype”. A supporting line reads “Fingerprint. PIN. On-device history.” Logo lands on strong cue 17.47s. Long settled ending; no end-to-black. Music fades over the final 1.5s.

All capabilities grounded in README and implemented UI. No claim of learned behavior, risk accuracy, guaranteed protection, production availability or store release. Sample app labels come from state.ts; no real user data is used. The native gate and success are an editorial demonstration of supported states. Build and validation run locally.
