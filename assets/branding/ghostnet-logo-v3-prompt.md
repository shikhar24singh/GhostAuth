## Exact prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create a substantially redesigned GhostNet Android app logo using image 1 only as the color reference and starting ghost idea. The user liked the original's appeal but found it generic, and strongly disliked a subsequent large G monogram. Do not use any letter or monogram.
Creative direction: a small elusive guardian spirit, drawn with the economy and character of a beautiful custom brand symbol. Design ONE compact, asymmetric floating ghost silhouette, leaning gently forward, with a rounded swept-back crown and a single elegant curling lower tail. A deep curved negative-space cut rises from the lower-right edge and curls through the body, giving the silhouette a distinctive fluid motion. Two small softly slanted navy eye cutouts give it a quietly alert, curious expression, not anger. The face and silhouette should work together as one memorable mark. Smooth deliberate curves, bold filled shape, minimal anchor-point feel. The shape should feel crafted and charming, with a clear head and tapered vapor tail; no arms, shield point, or scalloped sheet hem. Make it unmistakably a ghost, not a flame, bird, fox, letter, ninja hood or speech bubble.
Solid mint #68E2B9 on solid navy #101D25. Strictly flat two-color vector-like graphic. Center it at about 58 percent of a square canvas with generous margins. One app logo only, no presentation board.
Avoid: alphabet characters, shield outlines, padlocks, network nodes, circuit lines, fingerprint details, decorative rings, symmetry, stock clipart, aggressive esports styling, Snapchat shape, text, gradients, noise, glow, shadows, 3D, watermarks, rounded outer background corners.
