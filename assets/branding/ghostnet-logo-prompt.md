## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create one finished original logo icon for GhostNet, an Android app for adaptive authentication and protection of user-selected apps.
Design a bold, minimalist geometric symbol that fuses a ghost and a protective shield into ONE coherent silhouette. The ghost has a softly rounded head, two small deep-navy eye cutouts, and a simple lower outline that tapers into a shield-like point. It should feel calm, confident, intelligent and approachable, suitable for a serious security product. Make the silhouette distinctive and legible at very small sizes.
Style: exceptionally clean flat vector-like graphic, solid mint green (#68E2B9) symbol on a solid deep navy (#101D25) square background. Crisp edges, balanced curves, restrained geometry, generous negative space.
Composition: exactly one centered symbol, occupying approximately 60 percent of the square width and height, with comfortable safe margins for Android circular and squircle crops. Full bleed square navy background, no rounded outer corners baked into the image.
No text, no letters, no wordmark, no tagline, no padlock, no circuits, no network nodes, no extra symbols. No gradients, glow, shadows, bevels, 3D effects, texture, watermark, mockup or presentation board. Do not resemble the Snapchat ghost, a Halloween character or a gaming emblem. Deliver one polished square app icon.
